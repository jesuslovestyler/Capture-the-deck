# Capture the Deck

A free card game you play in your browser — two-player mode, played with a full 54-card deck including both Jokers. Outplay your opponent and take the whole deck.

**Live:** https://jesuslovestyler.github.io/Capture-the-deck/
**Home:** https://jesuslovestyler.com/

One self-contained page: no account, no ads, nothing to buy. Released into the public domain (Unlicense — see LICENSE). Built by Tyler with Noki.
