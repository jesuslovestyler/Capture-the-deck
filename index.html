How to play

  • A 54-card deck — the standard 52 plus two Jokers — is shuffled and split evenly, 27 cards each.
  • Each battle, you and Rex flip your top cards. The higher card wins, and the winner tucks every card in the pile underneath their deck.
  • Ranks run 2 (low) through 10, Jack, Queen, King, Ace (high). Suits never matter — only rank. Jokers beat everything, even Aces. Two Jokers tie, like any other pair.
  • War: on a tie, each side stakes 3 cards face down and flips one more — the higher decider takes the entire pile. Ties can chain into double and triple wars.
  • Short on cards for a war? You stake what you have and your last card becomes the decider. If you can't flip a card at all, you lose the game.
  • First side to hold all 54 cards wins. War can run long — flip on Auto and let it ride.
